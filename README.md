This repository contains time series at monthly and daily frequency of the refined high-frequency instruments for oil supply news shocks and the corresponding informational component used in Degasperi (2024). The instruments span the sample from 1983:7 to 2025:6. sp500_neg is the instrument to identify oil supply news shocks; sp500_pos identifies the information shock. Both instruments are based on the comovement with surprises in the S&P 500.
